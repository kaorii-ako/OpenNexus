## Product Hunt

OpenNexus is your personal AI morning briefing — self-hosted, private, works with local LLMs.

Connect Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Classroom, Notion, GitHub, Discord, RSS, and Weather. Every morning at 7 AM, your LLM synthesizes everything into a digest. Chat with your own data any time.

## Twitter/X Thread

```
1/ I built a self-hosted personal AI that reads your email, calendar, Notion, and GitHub every morning and gives you a briefing.

It runs locally (Ollama) so nothing leaves your machine.

Here's how it works 🧵

2/ Every morning at 7 AM, OpenNexus:
- Checks your Gmail
- Reads your calendar
- Scans open GitHub PRs
- Pulls Notion notes
- Gets weather
- Fetches RSS headlines

Then your local LLM synthesizes it into a morning briefing.

3/ You can also chat with your own data:

"What do I have this week?"
"Summarize my unread emails"
"What did I write in my notes about X?"

It uses RAG over your Notion workspace. Fully local.

4/ Setup:

pip install opennexus-ai
nexus init
nexus serve

The wizard walks you through connecting your services. Works with Ollama, OpenAI, or Anthropic.

5/ Stack: FastAPI + ChromaDB + SQLite + React. No cloud accounts required. MIT license.

GitHub ⭐: https://github.com/kaorii-ako/OpenNexus

Would love feedback from the self-hosting / local AI community.
```
